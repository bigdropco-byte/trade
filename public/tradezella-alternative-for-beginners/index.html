5 Essential Metrics Every Beginner Must Track (And What They Mean)

TradeZella inundates users with dozens of advanced quant statistics that confuse novice traders. TradeScrapbook highlights the 5 core numbers that actually dictate whether you survive your first year:

Win Rate Percentage

METRIC #1

The percentage of total closed trades that produced a net profit. A common beginner misconception is that you need an 80% win rate; in reality, 45% to 55% with positive risk-to-reward makes you profitable.

💡 TradeScrapbook Tip: We display your win rate alongside your average win and average loss so you never evaluate win rate in isolation.

Profit Factor

METRIC #2

Calculated as Gross Profits ÷ Gross Losses. A profit factor below 1.0 means you are losing money; between 1.2 and 1.6 indicates a healthy edge; above 2.0 represents exceptional system execution.

💡 TradeScrapbook Tip: If your profit factor is below 1.0, our AI audit tells you whether your issue is cutting winners early or letting losers run.

Average Risk-to-Reward (R:R)

METRIC #3

The ratio between your average dollar win and average dollar loss. If you risk $50 to make $100, your realized R:R is 2.0. Beginners frequently risk $100 to make $20, guaranteeing eventual liquidation.

💡 TradeScrapbook Tip: Instantly check your payoff ratio on our overview scorecard to confirm your winners outsize your losers.

Maximum Drawdown ($ & %)

METRIC #4

The largest peak-to-trough decline in account balance. Prop firms like FTMO and FundedNext fail traders immediately if daily drawdown reaches 5% or total drawdown reaches 10%.

💡 TradeScrapbook Tip: Our built-in drawdown monitor calculates your peak equity and alerts you if you approach danger zones.

Session & Weekday Edge

METRIC #5

Breaks down your profitability by London, New York, or Asian trading sessions, as well as by day of the week (Monday through Friday). Beginners often discover they bleed money on Fridays or during illiquid hours.

💡 TradeScrapbook Tip: One glance at our session heatmap shows you exactly which 2 hours of the day generate 90% of your profits.
